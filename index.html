Hi, I'm Carter.

I started making soft plastics three years ago after being introduced to remelts through the abundance of torn or ripped soft plastic baits. I started very small, only having access to a single mold, 2 colors, and a microwave to heat up my plastisol. I have now expanded to my own custom freeze dried worms, 5+ molds, a more efficient system to heat my plastisol (I was very happy to get one) over 10 color combinations, over 10 kinds of glitter, and various scents.
